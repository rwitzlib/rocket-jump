  • Rocket jump: look down, jump and fire at your feet. Your own rockets never hurt you.
  • Grapple walls, crates, vehicles, other players, even rockets in flight. The crosshair turns green in range.
  • Airshots: hitting someone in midair rings a bell and counts toward the Sky Hunter award.
  • Knock people off the map: a fall within 5 seconds of your hit is your kill.
  • ? boxes swap your launcher for a random one: grenades, the Hypersonic, or heat seekers.
  • First to the frag limit wins. Streaks, multi kills and shutdowns earn medals.
